SHUKAKU

一尾・守鶴 (THE SAND INCARNATION)

The One-Tail of Sunagakure, manifesting as a gigantic tanuki patterned with natural black cursed seal formula (Juinjutsu). Possessing pride as vast as the desert dunes, Shukaku controls sand with supernatural fluidity and unleashes deafening Wind Release air bullets capable of leveling rocky valleys.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Sunagakure (Hidden Sand)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Gaara (5th Kazekage) & Priest Bunpuku

Originally sealed inside a tea kettle and watched over by priest Bunpuku, Shukaku was later transferred into Gaara by Rasa to safeguard Sunagakure's borders. The bond evolved from violent insomnia into an enduring alliance during the Fourth Shinobi World War.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Magnet Release (Jiton): Harnesses innate magnetic chakra infused with ancient cursed seal scripts.
Sand Bullet Barrage: Pressurizes desert sand into devastating pneumatic artillery strikes.
Desert Layered Imperial Funeral: Encases colossal targets in miles of pressurized sand pyramids.

MATATABI

二尾・又旅 (THE BLUE FLAME MONSTER CAT)

A colossal feline beast composed entirely of engulfing cobalt-blue spiritual flames trimmed with black markings. Known as the Monster Cat (Bakeneko), Matatabi is renowned for its courteous speech, polite demeanor, and unmatched swiftness across difficult mountainous terrain.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Kumogakure (Hidden Cloud)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Yugito Nii (Kumo Jōnin Master)

Allocated to the Land of Lightning during the First Kage Summit. Through relentless tactical discipline, Yugito Nii achieved voluntary full-body transformation, earning high respect as Kumogakure's pride before facing Hidan and Kakuzu.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Ghostly Blue Fireball: Fires concentrated blue blaze spheres that explode into blazing pyres.
Flexible Claws & Agility: Shreds solid stone with flexible fiery claws while moving at blinding speeds.
Polite Demeanor: Unlike hostile beasts, Matatabi speaks with refined honorifics to fellow spirits.

ISOBU

三尾・磯撫 (THE GIANT ARMORED TURTLE)

A massive sea-dwelling turtle enveloped by an impenetrable spiky carapace and crab-like plating. Isobu rules the deep waters, generating tidal surges and hallucinogenic mist capable of confusing entire enemy divisions before crushing them in a rolling shell charge.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Kirigakure (Hidden Mist)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Yagura Karatachi (4th Mizukage) & Rin Nohara

Forced into Rin Nohara in a covert plot during the Third Shinobi War, Isobu later bonded with Fourth Mizukage Yagura Karatachi, who mastered full mutual control before being manipulated in the shadows by Obito Uchiha.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Coral Palm Infliction: Sprouts dense coral formations on targets to completely freeze their movement.
Shadow Rolling Shell: Curls into a colossal spiked sphere to crush entire coastlines.
Illusion Mist Deception: Releases dense fog that traps pursuers in waking nightmare genjutsu.

SON GOKŪ

四尾・孫悟空 (THE MONKEY KING OF SAGE CAVES)

The proud Monkey King (Suitentei) of the Water Screen Cave. Son Gokū resembles a red-furred, green-skinned gorilla wielding the devastating power of Lava Release (Yōton), capable of spitting molten boulders and incinerating entire battlefield sectors.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Iwagakure (Hidden Stone)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Rōshi (Veteran Hermit Shinobi)

Allocated to Iwagakure during the First Kage Summit. Rōshi spent over forty years wandering as an ascetic hermit attempting to understand the beast's true essence before discovering mutual respect alongside Naruto Uzumaki.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Lava Release Scorching: Melts subterranean rock into torrents of boiling magma at will.
Monkey King Taijutsu: Deploys colossal brute strength to grapple even the largest summon monsters.
Great Flower-Fruit Mountain: Detonates a volcanic eruption directly underneath enemy forces.

KOKUŌ

五尾・穆王 (THE WHITE ILLUSION STEED)

A magnificent hybrid beast possessing the body of a white horse and the crested head of a dolphin. Kokuō channels Boil Release (Futton) to heat its chakra to boiling point, generating colossal steam propulsion that drives its horn charges through steel barriers.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Iwagakure (Hidden Stone)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Han (The Steam Shinobi Titan)

Partnered with Han in Iwagakure, Kokuō utilized steam armor to become a living battering ram during border skirmishes. Following its release after the Fourth War, Kokuō chose a secluded, peaceful forest existence away from human conflict.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Boil Release Propelled Charge: Melts steam pressure to accelerate ramming horns with mountain-shattering force.
Horn Breaker Impact: Physical impacts capable of sending Eight-Tails skidding hundreds of meters.
Quiet Pride: Values calm natural balance, communicating softly and avoiding pointless war.

SAIKEN

六尾・犀犬 (THE GIANT SLIME SLUG)

A colossal, white, six-tailed slug covered in a thick layer of viscous, corrosive secretion. Saiken exudes lethal acidic gas and caustic slime that instantaneously dissolves flesh, steel armor, and ninjutsu barriers on contact.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Kirigakure (Hidden Mist)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Utakata (Rogue Bubble Shinobi)

Sealed within Utakata in Kirigakure, a tragic misunderstanding with master Harusame caused Utakata to flee into exile. Utakata fused Saiken's corrosive fluids with Soap Bubble Ninjutsu (Shabondama) before being captured by Pain's Six Paths.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Corrosive Gas Spray: Expels caustic vapor capable of melting entire fortress gates in seconds.
Viscous Mucus Trapping: Exudes adhesive fluid to anchor, disarm, and suffocate enemy vanguards.
Soft Elastic Form: Absorbs tremendous kinetic impacts by deforming its gelatinous mass.

CHŌMEI

七尾・重明 (THE LUCKY SEVEN KABUTOMUSHI)

Resembling a colossal armored rhinoceros beetle with six insect wings and one tail making up its seven appendages. Nicknamed "Lucky Seven Chōmei", it is the only Bijuu capable of sustained supersonic flight, firing luminous blinding scale powders.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Takigakure (Hidden Waterfall)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Fū (The Spirited Kunoichi)

The sole Bijuu possessed by a minor hidden village (Takigakure). Fū grew up sheltered by village leader Shibuki and sought to befriend one hundred allies during the joint Chūnin Exams before encountering the Akatsuki.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Blinding Scale Powder: Releases radiant scales that blind enemy ocular sensors and Sharingan.
Supersonic Aerial Ramming: Dives at extreme velocities using razor-hard beetle horns.
Cocoon Regeneration: Weaves dense chakra threads to form protective healing cocoons.

GYŪKI

八尾・牛鬼 (THE GIANT OX-CEPHALOPOD)

A colossal cephalopod-ox hybrid (Ushi-oni) possessing four muscular horns and eight massive octopus tentacles for tails. Boasting monumental strength second only to Kurama, Gyūki commands blinding ink seals and colossal 360-degree tail twisters.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Kumogakure (Hidden Cloud)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Killer B (Brother of the 4th Raikage) & Blue B

After multiple tragic host failures in Kumogakure, young Killer B matched Gyūki's spirit with pure mutual brotherhood, becoming the world's most renowned Perfect Jinchūriki and master instructor of Tailed Beast cooperation.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Tailed Beast Eight Twister: Spins octopus tails with hurricane force to flatten surrounding forests.
Sealing Ink Matrix: Expels high-density ink clones that bind and immobilize enemy squads.
Rapid Regenerative Tentacles: Severed tentacles regrow swiftly and can serve as decoy body doubles.

KURAMA

九尾・九喇嘛 (THE NINE-TAILED DEMON FOX)

The strongest of the nine Tailed Beasts, Kurama is a colossal nine-tailed Kitsune whose chakra reserves dwarf the other eight beasts combined. Once viewed as an avatar of hatred, Kurama forged a historic bond with Naruto Uzumaki, becoming the ultimate savior of the shinobi world.

VILLAGE ✕ JINCHŪRIKI RELATIONSHIP
HISTORICAL VILLAGE Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf)
PRIMARY JINCHŪRIKI Naruto Uzumaki, Kushina, Mito, Minato

Originally sealed by Hashirama into Mito Uzumaki, transferred to Kushina, and divided into Yin/Yang halves by Minato Namikaze. Through Naruto's unwavering empathy, Kurama shed centuries of hatred to unlock Kurama Mode and save reality from Kaguya Ōtsutsuki.

SIGNATURE MYTHOLOGY & COMBAT TRAITS

Negative Emotion Sensing: Detects malicious intent and hidden chakra across entire continents.
Planetary Tailed Beast Ball: Obliterates entire mountain ranges with a single super-compressed blast.
Baryon Mode Fusion: Fuses Naruto and Kurama's chakra nuclei into raw nuclear energy to drain rival lifespans.
